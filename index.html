✳REFLECTION 001MY STARTING POINT

Why I chose AI Engineering

I chose AI Engineering because I believe artificial intelligence has a lot of potential and will continue to develop in the years ahead. What interests me is that the field is still evolving. There are many ways AI can be improved, and there are still problems that need better solutions.

I want to learn how AI systems work, what makes them useful, and how to develop technology that can help people. I know that becoming skilled in this field will take time, especially because I'm only starting college. For now, my goal is to understand the fundamentals, stay open to learning, and build my skills through my classes and future projects.

I'm not expecting to know everything right away. I see this portfolio as a way to track my progress, keep examples of what I create, and look back on how my understanding changes throughout college.
